5 optimal times to drink Atlas.
You have your Atlas. Now here is how to get the most from every single sachet.

1. Morning Hydration
You lose up to a liter of water overnight through breathing and sweat. Start your day with Atlas and 12-16oz of water to replenish what you lost. Your body absorbs electrolytes faster on an empty stomach.

2. Pre-Workout and During Exercise
Drink Atlas 30 minutes before your workout for sustained energy without the sugar crash. During intense exercise, your body loses sodium and potassium rapidly — Atlas replaces both at clinically relevant levels.

3. Travel and Flights
Cabin air sits at roughly 4% humidity — drier than the Sahara. This is exactly why Atlas was created. Drink one sachet before boarding and another mid-flight. Your body will thank you when you land.

4. Long Workdays
That afternoon slump is often dehydration, not hunger. Mix a sachet into your water bottle and sip through the afternoon. The B vitamins (B6, B12, Niacin, Pantothenic Acid) support natural energy production.

5. After Alcohol
Alcohol is a diuretic — it pulls water and electrolytes from your body. One sachet before bed and one in the morning can help your body recover faster. The L-Glutamine and magnesium support recovery.
