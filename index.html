Founder's Story

Created by a Pilot

Atlas Hydration was born at 35,000 feet. Garrett Ray is a commercial airline pilot for American Airlines — gone 15 to 17 days a month, constantly crossing time zones, breathing dry cabin air that strips moisture from your body faster than you realize. Growing up with a father who flew for 36 years, Garrett knew the toll aviation takes on the body.

After years of searching for a hydration solution that actually worked — without the sugar, artificial ingredients, or watered-down formulas — Garrett decided to create his own. He spent months researching electrolyte science, working with formulators, and testing blends until he developed the perfect ratio: 1,769mg of total electrolytes, essential B vitamins, Vitamin C, and recovery amino acids — all with zero sugar and only 5 calories.

The result is Atlas — a premium electrolyte mix built for pilots, athletes, travelers, and anyone who demands more from their hydration. A portion of every purchase helps provide clean water to communities in need around the world.
