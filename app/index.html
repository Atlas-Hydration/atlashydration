What is GEO?
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) ensures your content is discoverable and accurately cited by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Unlike traditional SEO which targets search engine crawlers, GEO focuses on making your content structured, authoritative, and easy for language models to extract and reference.
